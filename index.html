What's inside

Everything you need to train smarter

From logging your first set to advanced strength analytics — Trainix grows with you.

Log Sets in Seconds

Ghost-text pre-fills your last weight and reps, with an auto rest timer between sets. Confirm and log — minimal typing.

150+ Exercise Library

Search by muscle, equipment, and movement type. How-to guides for exercises, plus unlimited custom movements with Pro.

Automatic PR Tracking

Trainix detects new personal records after every session — estimated 1RM, max weight, max volume, and max reps.

Training Plans

Follow built-in programs day by day, or generate a personalized plan with Pro. Progress advances automatically after each session.

Analytics & Coach Insights

Free: streaks, consistency, workout frequency, and PR history. Pro unlocks volume trends, strength progress, and movement breakdowns.

Trainix Coach PRO

In-app coaching chat tailored to your logged workouts — programming, nutrition, recovery, technique, and suggested questions.

Nutrition Tracking

Log meals from a built-in food database and custom foods. Pro adds barcode scanning, macro goals, and AI food photo logging.

Workout Share Cards

After each session, share a polished recap image with stats, PRs, and session summary — ready for WhatsApp, Instagram, and more.

Habits & Body Metrics

Track daily habits with streaks, log body weight and body fat, and unlock full measurements, RPE per set, and trends with Pro.

Health Sync & Backup

Export workouts to Apple Health or Health Connect. Back up and restore locally as JSON/ZIP, with CSV export on Pro.

Privacy first

Your training data stays yours

  • Stored on your device

    Workouts, PRs, plans, and metrics are saved locally using SQLite — not on a server.

  • No account required

    Start logging workouts immediately. No sign-up, no email, no password.

  • Export anytime

    Create a full JSON backup of your training history whenever you want, and restore it on any device.

  • Health sync is optional

    Apple Health and Health Connect integration is entirely opt-in and can be turned off at any time.

Built to keep your data private

No cloud sync, no third-party data sales — your training history belongs to you.
